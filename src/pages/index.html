Shipwreck chic

Built where boats were built.

Stowaway Labs lives in the former TideRunner Boat Co. building, tucked under Devoted Kiss Cafe on Harborview Drive.

We took our cues from the generations of boatbuilders, fishers, loggers, and settlers who made this harbor what it is. Oars on the walls, an upturned rowboat for a bookshelf, sea-glass booths, and windows full of water. It is a place to focus, find your flow, and be around other people building a future.

A sense of adventure doesn’t hurt, either.
