Good questions

Before you come aboard

Can I bring a colleague or my team?

Yes. When you book, choose 1 to 6 people and we will seat you together. Drop-in guests are $12 per person per hour, and members can cover themselves with their membership. Corporate Flex teams draw from one shared pool of 100 hours a month.

What are the house rules?

Stowaway Labs is for adults 18 and older, there are no pets, everyone checks in on arrival, and members get priority seating.

What kinds of desks are there?

Monitor desks with ultrawide screens, no-monitor desks, heated table seats, and booth seats. Pick your favorite when you book.

How do I manage my membership?

Members book and manage billing through our member portal. Use the Member login link at the top of any page.

Is there really a free day?

There is. Locals in greater Gig Harbor can spend a full day with every amenity, free. Claim yours here.
