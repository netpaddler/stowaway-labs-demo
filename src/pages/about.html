Our story

Built by locals, for the curious.

Stowaway Labs is a creative and entrepreneurial hub for the greater Gig Harbor region. A place for people to do their best work and host their best events.

The founders

Meet Nick and Lauren Gough.

Stowaway Labs was launched by local entrepreneurs Nick and Lauren Gough.

After the success of the marketing software company he founded in 2018, Nick wanted a way to give back to the Gig Harbor community. Together with his wife Lauren, he set out to build a place where local creators, entrepreneurs, and remote workers could find community, blaze their own trail, and work through challenges alongside people just like themselves.

On September 22, 2025, the doors opened on Harborview Drive.

From boat shop to workshop

A building with salt in its bones.

Stowaway Labs lives in the former TideRunner Boat Co. building, under Devoted Kiss Cafe on Gig Harbor’s historic waterfront.

We are inspired by the generations of boatbuilders, fishers, loggers, and settlers who shaped this harbor. So we kept the spirit of the place: wood and rope, oars and charts, an upturned rowboat for a bookshelf, and big windows pointed at the water. We call the look shipwreck chic.
