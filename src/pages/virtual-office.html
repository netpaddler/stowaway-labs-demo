Virtual office FAQ

What does it cost?

$49 per month, or $39 per month for coworking members, on a 12-month term. There is a one-time $30 setup fee. Full-time members get virtual office service included.

Do you scan or forward mail?

Not yet. For now, you pick up your mail in person, Monday to Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. We email you each day something arrives.

How big a package can you hold?

Packages up to 6 cubic feet or 30 pounds.

Can I list more than one name?

Yes. Up to three names or DBAs can receive mail at your mailbox.

How do I sign up?
